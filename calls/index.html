How scoring works

Graded in public

A record settles each call

Every call names the published record that decides it and the date by which it resolves.

A stated likelihood

Likelihoods follow a fixed scale: very likely means 80–95%, likely 55–80%, about even 45–55%, and so on down.

The Brier score

Each resolved call scores the square of the gap between its stated chance and what happened. 0 is perfect; always saying 50% scores 0.25.

Nothing is rewritten

Calls are never deleted or reworded. An update is a new, dated call, so the old one stays on the record.
