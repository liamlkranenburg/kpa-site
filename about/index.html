Standards

What readers can count on

Corrections

Errors are fixed on the brief's page, with the date and a note of what changed. Reports go to the corrections inbox.

Independence

Self-funded. No sponsors, clients or advertisers, and not paid by anyone it writes about.

Not advice

Research for information only. KPA is not a registered investment adviser and has no clients; nothing here is investment, legal or regulatory advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

AI disclosure

Briefs are researched and drafted with AI assistance. Every number is still recomputed from public data and checked by hand.

Reuse

Quote and link freely with a citation. Each brief page has a ready-made one. Please ask before republishing a whole brief.
