Numbers the brief stands on

43%

At the peaks of winter 2025–26, California's battery fleet could have cut peak net load by at most 43% of its rating, against 72% at summer 2025's peak.

KPA tested how far each of the six largest battery fleets, as they stood in October 2026, could have pushed down each season's peak net load. California's could have cut the winter peak by 43% of its rating and the summer one by 72%; the four large Southwest utilities' fleets, by 47% and 76%. NERC counts batteries in both regions at 85%.

KPA's own calculation from EIA-930 hourly data and EIA Form 860M (October 2026), compared with NERC's assessment-area counts. The method follows Denholm and others (NREL, 2020) and Mills and Rodriguez (LBNL, 2020), and the modelled ELCC studies by Astrapé for ERCOT and E3 for the Desert Southwest are credited; the regional winter and summer limits were not found in KPA's prior-publication search.

On the desk

What could change the numbers

Releases that could move a published finding. When one lands, the finding is checked again.

  • 30 Nov 2026

    Stanton 1 emergency order expires

    DOE Order 202-26-42 keeps Orlando's Stanton Unit 1 available through 30 November; no renewal had been posted by 25 November. Stanton 1 ran at 43% in January–August 2026 under orders. See the finding

  • 1 Dec 2026

    Winter 2026–27 begins, the season KPA's calls test

    KPA call CS-1: across ERCOT's 10 highest net-load hours, December 2026 to February 2027, battery output averages less than 30% of battery capacity in service (Likely, 70%). It was 24% in winter 2025 and 7% in 2025–26. See the finding

  • 3 Dec 2026

    GE Vernova CEO at Goldman Sachs conference

    First public remarks since the third-quarter release, when gas backlog and slot reservations stood at 122 GW. KPA call SM-1 needs about 8 GW more by year-end. See the finding

  • 9 Dec 2026

    PJM 2029/30 capacity auction opens

    Bidding runs 9–15 December under a $337.83/MW-day cap and $181.91 floor, with a 158,712 MW reliability requirement. It settles KPA call LB-1: a clear at the price cap. See the finding

  • 11 Dec 2026

    Centralia 2 emergency order expires

    DOE Order 202-26-44 keeps TransAlta's Centralia Unit 2 available through 11 December. The unit ran at 0% in January–August 2026. See the finding

  • 18 Dec 2026

    Schahfer and Culley emergency orders expire

    DOE Orders 202-26-46 and 202-26-47 hold 812 MW in Indiana through 18 December. If Schahfer's 722 MW retired this month along with Sherco 1 and Comanche 2, 2026 coal retirements would pass 1.5 GW and KPA call BF-1 would fail. See the finding
