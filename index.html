From Banked Fires

Numbers the brief stands on

Select a stone to read the finding, its source, and the page it comes from.

22%

Coal held open by federal order ran at 22% of capacity in January–August 2026; coal its owners kept ran at 38%.

KPA matched every delayed unit to DOE's orders and its owner's filings, then measured its output in EIA's generator data through August. The units held by orders ran at 22% of capacity in January–August 2026, down from 49% in the same months of 2022–24. Units their owners kept ran at 38%, up from 34%, against 47% for the US coal fleet.

KPA's comparison of ordered units, owner-kept units and the fleet, from EIA-923 and EIA Form 860M data (fleet figure from EIA's Electric Power Monthly). Falling output at the ordered plants was reported earlier by Utility Dive (23 June 2026), Argus (30 March 2026) and MV2 (19 October 2026), and is credited.

What could change the numbers

Releases that could move a published finding. When one lands, the finding is checked again.

  • 14 Nov 2026

    Campbell's sixth emergency order expires

    DOE has renewed the J.H. Campbell order five times since May 2025, and the one court ruling so far vacated an order that had already expired. A seventh renewal fits KPA call BF-3: Campbell still listed as operating in March 2027. See the finding

  • 9 Dec 2026

    PJM 2029/30 capacity auction opens

    Bidding runs 9–15 December, with results due around 22 December. It settles KPA call LB-1: a clear at the price cap. See the finding

  • Expected December

    Grid Strategies 2026 load forecast

    Resets the 166 GW and 90 GW forecast baselines that the flexibility findings are measured against. Earlier editions came out in December. See the finding

    Watching
  • January 2027

    GE Vernova fourth-quarter results

    Settles KPA call SM-1: at least 130 GW of gas orders and reservations at year-end. GE guided to about 13 GW of new agreements in the fourth quarter. See the finding

    Ruling on DOE's rehearing petition in Michigan v. DOE

    The D.C. Circuit vacated DOE's first Campbell order on 11 September 2026, and DOE has asked for a rehearing. A ruling that sets aside an order while it is in force would change KPA's view. See the finding

    Watching

    EIA Form 860M for December 2026

    Settles KPA call BF-1: less than 1.5 GW of US coal retires in 2026. Of the 3.6 GW still dated for November and December, 2.6 GW is under federal orders. See the finding
